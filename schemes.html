Help & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check if I am eligible for Rythu Bandhu?
You must be a farmer with patta land in Telangana. Log in to the portal, go to the Rythu Bandhu section, enter your Aadhaar number or patta number, and the system will verify your eligibility automatically.
Can I apply for multiple schemes simultaneously?
Yes. You can apply for multiple schemes at the same time through your citizen dashboard. Each scheme has its own eligibility criteria and processing timeline.
What documents are required for Aasara Pension?
You need: Aadhaar card, ration card (white/pink), age proof, bank passbook (first page), caste certificate (for SC/ST), and disability certificate (if applicable for differently-abled category).
How long does the Aarogyasri enrollment take?
Aarogyasri enrollment is typically completed within 2 working days after successful verification of your White Ration Card details and Aadhaar. The card is delivered to your registered address.
Is the 2BHK scheme open for applications now?
The 2BHK scheme has specific application windows. Check the notices section or call our helpline 1800-599-4747 for the current application status in your district.
How do I report an issue with scheme payment?
You can raise a grievance through your citizen dashboard under 'My Grievances', call our 24/7 helpline 1800-599-4747, or visit your nearest Sachivalayam center with your application reference number.
